ABOUT THE VAULT

Our history. Our stories. Our culture.

A living record of LiteFeet, shaped by the people who made it.

FROM THE FOUNDER

Dear LiteFeet,

LiteFeet is more than dance. It's a culture built through creativity, individuality, competition, community, and the people who have continued to push it forward.

Over the years, the culture has created countless moments, memories, movements, and contributions that deserve to be remembered.

But not everything has been documented. Not every name has been recognized. Not every story has been told.

That's part of why I created The LiteFeet Vault.

I wanted to build a space where our history could be preserved, where our contributions could be acknowledged, and where the community could have a role in telling its own story.

This isn't just about looking back. It's about documenting what's happening now, connecting the past to the present, and creating something future generations can learn from.

The Vault is meant to continue growing alongside the culture. It won't be complete overnight, and it shouldn't depend on any one person's perspective.

Because LiteFeet's history belongs to the people who made it.

With love for the culture,TeeFounder, The LiteFeet Vault

Our Mission

Document, preserve and celebrate LiteFeet culture through historical records, community knowledge, recognition and sourced storytelling—connecting the past, present and future.

About the Archive

The Vault brings together LiteFeet history, people, teams, battles, events, music and awards. We are building toward a connected cultural archive and editorial platform.

How It Works

Historical sources and community contributions help build the record. Contributions are reviewed before publication; corrections keep the archive accountable to the culture.
