DANCER'S CHOICE AWARDS

The community chooses.

The Dancer’s Choice Awards were created in response to a simple concern: LiteFeet community members wanted a meaningful say in who received recognition. While the LiteFeet Awards have their own awards process, DCA lets the culture shape its categories, nominate the people it believes deserve recognition, and vote for the final winners.

The idea is to give people a constructive way to recognize dancers, creators, crews, and contributors they feel were overlooked. Recognition should not end with one awards ceremony.

How DCA works

  1. Suggest categories: community members help decide what should be recognized.
  2. Nominate: suggest an eligible person or add another nomination to someone already listed. Each nomination increases that candidate's total.
  3. Top five: the five most-nominated eligible candidates per category advance to the final voting round.
  4. Vote: the community selects the winner from those finalists once voting opens.

Nominations are designed to continue beyond LiteFeet Awards weekend, giving people time to turn disagreements about recognition into support for their own choices. The exact nomination closing date will be announced separately.
