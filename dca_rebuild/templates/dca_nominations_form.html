NOMINATIONS

{% include "dca_eligibility_notice.html" %}

THIS IS THE NOMINATION ROUND, NOT THE FINAL VOTE. You're suggesting who deserves to be a finalist. Nothing you submit here counts as a vote for the award winner.

  1. Pick a category and nominate a dancer, producer, crew, creator or other eligible candidate.
  2. You can nominate someone who is already listed. Each valid nomination adds one to that candidate's nomination total. For example, 8 nominations means 8 nominations of support, not 8 winning votes.
  3. Submit one nomination per category every rolling 24 hours. You can nominate in other available categories while waiting.
  4. The top five eligible nominees in each category become the finalists. Only then does the community vote for the winner.

This process gives the community a chance to recognize people who may have been overlooked. The nomination period is intended to continue beyond LiteFeet Awards weekend; the final closing date will be announced separately.
