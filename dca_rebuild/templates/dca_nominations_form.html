The community chose the first 25 award categories. You can help nominate the people who represent LiteFeet—and help strong additional categories join the awards.

  1. Explore the Top 25 categories one at a time. Skip any that don't apply to you.
  2. You get one nomination per New York calendar day, across all categories. Return tomorrow to submit another.
  3. Choose an already-nominated dancer or suggest a new one. Approved nominees and their support totals appear on each category.
  4. The five most-nominated eligible people or teams per official category will advance to final voting. Rankings are provisional until nominations close.
  5. After the Top 25, explore additional categories and support write-ins. Write-ins may qualify to expand the awards list.

Your email is used for daily limits and duplicate prevention; it isn't displayed publicly. Submitted names are reviewed before appearing on public lists.
