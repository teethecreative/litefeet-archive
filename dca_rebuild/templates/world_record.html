THE LITEFEET VAULT / COMMUNITY INITIATIVES

Let's Make LiteFeet History.

We're bringing the LiteFeet community together to plan a synchronized dance world record attempt rooted in Harlem. We need the people who dance the culture and the people who help make it possible.

This is a proposed community-led record attempt. No record title, venue, date, or participant threshold has been approved by Guinness World Records.

01 / THE VISION

One culture. One routine. One community.

LiteFeet has always been shaped by its dancers, teams, creators, music and Harlem roots. This initiative aims to bring people together across generations for one coordinated performance, and to document the work, people and history behind it.

We're in the planning and recruitment stage. The choreography, official record application, rehearsal locations, date and main venue are still being developed.

03 / THE PROCESS

How we prepare.

  1. Build the choreography. Collaborative development followed by one approved final version.
  2. Train the captains. Share consistent music, counts, tutorials and teaching instructions.
  3. Open local rehearsals. Organize approved spaces so groups can practice the same routine.
  4. Bring the community together. Coordinate combined rehearsals and event-day verification after official requirements are confirmed.
