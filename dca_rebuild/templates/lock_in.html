Lock In

Build a round the way you’d build one on the floor. Pick your dancer, find the right track, and put your moves together before you call “locked in.” Lock In brings LiteFeet dancers, songs, moves, and battle spaces to the table.

How to play

How to battle

A game is called a battle. Battles are 1v1 (dancer versus dancer), 2v2 (tag team), or 3v3 (team battle). Win by taking the most rounds, reaching the required Battle Points (BP), or landing enough TKOs.

Winning a round

A round averages 100 Battle Points (BP). The player with the most points wins. It also lists a TKO, an opponent unable to draw, or an opponent producing a tweak as potential win conditions. Dropping below 50 BP means an automatic loss.

Winning a battle

1v1 and 2v2 play two rounds; 3v3 plays three. A battle also ends if your opponent has no cards left to play. Final BP and TKO targets will be announced.

Preparing to battle

  1. Greet your opponent, shuffle and cut both decks; you may also shuffle and cut your opponent's deck.
  2. Place decks face down in their designated, color-coordinated zones.
  3. Spin the adidas counter. The winner chooses the arena and whether to act first or second. In the next battle, the previous winner selects who begins; after a tie, spin again.

Battle conditions

A player may challenge an invalid card play using the manual. The person whose challenge or play is wrong loses 50 BP for that round.

1v1 round structure

  1. Choose a dancer and song card and put them in their zones. Use an arena card and the song's documented effects.
  2. Draw seven cards. Your hand can contain moves, upgrades and fribs.
  3. Phase 1: setup moves, such as Rev Up, Bad One, or Tone Whop.
  4. Phase 2: place up to three move cards face down.
  5. Phase 3: announce “locked in” before the other player starts. A called-out missed announcement costs 10 BP.
  6. Apply trick, arena, song, spell and frib effects and compare results; use a Round of Fire if tied, pending final tie rules.

2v2 and 3v3

Tag-team and team-battle modes are coming soon.

Card types

Build a 25–50 card deck with dancers, arenas, songs, moves, tricks, upgrades, fribs and other support cards. Producers are credited on Song Cards, not played as separate cards.
