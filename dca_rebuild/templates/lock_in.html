How to play

Adapted directly from the Litefeet Card Planning — Official Rule Book Sample. Blank Battle Point thresholds and unfinished team-battle steps remain under development.

How to battle

A game is called a battle. Battles are 1v1 (dancer versus dancer), 2v2 (tag team), or 3v3 (team battle). The planning draft describes a battle winner by most rounds, a not-yet-specified BP total, or a not-yet-specified number of TKOs.

Winning a round

The rulebook uses an average of 100 Battle Points (BP) for a round. The player with the most points wins. It also lists a TKO, an opponent unable to draw, or an opponent producing a tweak as potential win conditions. A player below 50 BP automatically loses under the draft rule.

Winning a battle

1v1 and 2v2 are drafted as two-round formats; 3v3 as three rounds. Final BP targets and the exact TKO requirement are blank in the rulebook. Running out of playable cards is also listed as a loss condition. The interactive prototype does not invent missing thresholds.

Preparing to battle

  1. Greet your opponent, shuffle and cut both decks; you may also shuffle and cut your opponent's deck.
  2. Place decks face down in their designated, color-coordinated zones.
  3. Spin the adidas counter. The winner chooses the arena and whether to act first or second. In the next battle, the previous winner selects who begins; after a tie, spin again.

Battle conditions

A player may challenge an invalid card play using the manual. The person whose challenge or play is wrong loses 50 BP for that round.

1v1 round structure

  1. Choose a dancer and song card and put them in their zones. Use an arena card, with producer effects when available.
  2. Draw a seven-card hand. The draft includes move, spell/upgrade, and frib cards.
  3. Phase 1: setup moves, such as Rev Up, Bad One, or Tone Whop.
  4. Phase 2: place up to three move cards face down.
  5. Phase 3: announce “locked in” before the other player starts. A called-out missed announcement costs 10 BP.
  6. Apply trick, arena, song, spell and frib effects and compare results; use a Round of Fire if tied, pending final tie rules.

2v2 and 3v3

The draft identifies 2v2 card swaps and 3v3 team play, but their complete sequence is not yet written. These formats are shown as rulebook previews, not falsely playable as finalized battles.

Card types

Use 25–50 total cards from dancers, arenas, producers, songs, moves, tricks, upgrades/spells, fribs and team support. The card builder below enforces total-card and copy limits. The individual card-effect values that are unfinished in the document remain unscored.

Be part of Lock In

Dancers can pitch five stats, their strongest stat, a signature move, and what it should do in a battle. Producers can send tracks grouped as Common, Rare, and Legendary. You can also suggest an arena, explain a team or song connection, or volunteer to playtest.

Email Tee with your LiteFeet name and what you’d like to contribute. Mention if you want to review your card before it’s finalized.
