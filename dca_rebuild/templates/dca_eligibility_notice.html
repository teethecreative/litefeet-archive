Nomination qualifications update

Following community requests, every new nomination must include a reason explaining why the nominee deserves recognition in the selected category.

Nominees should have relevant LiteFeet activity or contributions between November 2025 and November 2026. Past reputation alone does not establish eligibility.

Existing nominees without recorded explanations may be disqualified from the final ballot unless qualifying reasoning is submitted and reviewed. No nominee is automatically disqualified for missing information.
