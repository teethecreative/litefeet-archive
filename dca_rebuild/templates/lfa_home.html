THE LITEFEET VAULT / AWARDS

LITEFEET
AWARDS

The LiteFeet Awards are an established awards program recognizing achievements, contributions, and milestones in LiteFeet culture. Like a traditional awards ceremony, the program honors standout dancers, creators, crews, and community figures through its own recognition process.

The Dancer's Choice Awards are a separate, community-led program where people help select categories, nominate their choices, and vote for the winners. These two programs should not be confused.

Historical LiteFeet Awards categories, nominees, and winners are being gathered and verified. The Vault distinguishes documented results from community submissions awaiting review.

THE HISTORICAL ARCHIVE

LITEFEET AWARDS HISTORY

The Vault preserves documented LiteFeet Awards categories, honorees, and results. Historical records are reviewed and distinguished from unverified information.

The LiteFeet Awards are selected through S and the awards panel, independently of The LiteFeet Vault. This page is for viewing award history, not requesting nominations or suggesting recipients. Public additions to this awards archive are closed.
