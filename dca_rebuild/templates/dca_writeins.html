WRITE-IN CATEGORIES

All eligible community-suggested categories outside the Top 25 are open for nomination support while nominations are active. Choose a category to nominate someone or support a nominee already listed. These are not final votes.

HOW WRITE-INS WORK

  1. Find a category. Search the community's submitted write-in categories below.
  2. Nominate someone. Pick a category, then submit a candidate's name. You can support someone already nominated; each valid nomination adds to their total.
  3. Build support. Write-ins must receive at least {{ requirement }} distinct supporters, more unique support than the least-supported official Top 25 category, and at least five eligible nominees.
  4. Qualification. When nominations close, categories that meet the requirements can enter the award program. Nomination totals are not votes for the winner.

One nomination per category per email address every rolling 24 hours. Category access is separate from final qualification. All categories remain subject to the published participation rules.
