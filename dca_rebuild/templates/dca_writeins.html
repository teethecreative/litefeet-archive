How Write-Ins Work

  1. Choose a write-in category.
  2. Nominate. Add a new nominee or support an existing one.
  3. Build support. A category needs at least {{ requirement }} different supporters, more supporters than the least-supported Top 25 category, and five eligible nominees.
  4. Review. Categories that meet the requirements are reviewed after nominations close.

One nomination per category every rolling 24 hours. Being open for nominations doesn't mean a category has qualified. All categories remain subject to the participation rules.
