A LETTER TO THE COMMUNITY

Dear LiteFeet,

LiteFeet is more than dance. It's a culture built through creativity, individuality, competition, community, and the people who have continued to push it forward.

Over the years, the culture has created countless moments, memories, movements, and contributions that deserve to be remembered.

But not everything has been documented. Not every name has been recognized. Not every story has been told.

That's part of why I created The LiteFeet Vault.

I wanted to build a space where our history could be preserved, where our contributions could be acknowledged, and where the community could have a role in telling its own story.

This isn't just about looking back. It's about documenting what's happening now, connecting the past to the present, and creating something future generations can learn from.

The Vault is meant to continue growing alongside the culture. It won't be complete overnight, and it shouldn't depend on any one person's perspective.

Because LiteFeet's history belongs to the people who made it.

With love for the culture,

Tee Founder, The LiteFeet Vault

THE BEGINNING / ORIGINS

Where the idea began

The Vault started with a problem: too much of our history lives in memories, scattered videos, old conversations, and posts that can disappear. The culture has continued to grow, but preserving its history hasn't always kept pace. The goal is to bring those pieces together without losing their context.

BEYOND THE DANCE / CULTURE

A culture bigger than its highlights

LiteFeet isn't only the moves people recognize. It's the dancers, teams, battles, music, events, creativity, competition, and community relationships surrounding them. The Vault connects these parts instead of treating each one like an isolated moment.

THE PEOPLE & VOICES / COMMUNITY

History belongs to the people who lived it

Dancers, producers, teams, hosts, judges, organizers, spectators, and others have all contributed to LiteFeet. Their experiences matter, including stories that aren't widely known. The Vault makes room for multiple perspectives without treating every account as an automatically verified fact.

ROOTS & REACH / PLACES

The places that carry the culture

From Harlem and across New York City to communities farther away, LiteFeet's story is connected to the spaces where people gathered, practiced, performed, and competed. The Vault documents those connections while distinguishing established history from stories that still need research.

THEN, NOW & NEXT / GENERATIONS

Not just a look back

The Vault connects earlier generations with the dancers and events shaping the culture today. Historical records, upcoming gatherings, corrections, and newly discovered material all contribute to an evolving picture. A record can grow as additional sources become available.

BUILDING THE RECORD / PRESERVATION

A record we build with care

Community members can contribute information, photographs, footage, firsthand accounts, and corrections. Submissions are reviewed before publication. Sources, conflicting accounts, and unresolved questions remain visible where appropriate, because preserving history doesn't mean pretending every question has one settled answer.
