HOW IT WORKS

EVERY CONTRIBUTION HELPS.

  1. EXPLORE PRESERVED RECORDS AND THEIR ORIGINAL SOURCES.
  2. SUBMIT ADDITIONAL MATERIAL, A FIRSTHAND ACCOUNT, OR A CORRECTION.
  3. THE VAULT REVIEWS SOURCES AND CONTRIBUTIONS BEFORE PUBLICATION.
  4. RECORDS CAN BE UPDATED WITH ATTRIBUTED EVIDENCE WHILE PRESERVING HISTORICAL CONTEXT.

COMMUNITY INPUT—TRUE, FALSE, OR DEBATABLE—HELPS IDENTIFY CLAIMS THAT NEED CLOSER REVIEW. IT DOES NOT REPLACE VERIFICATION.
