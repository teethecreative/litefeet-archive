LiteFeet Awards

LiteFeet Awards Weekend

The LiteFeet Awards is a full weekend dedicated to celebrating LiteFeet as a culture, not just as a dance style. It usually runs Friday through Sunday with a press conference, a battle event, and the awards ceremony.

The press conference gives the community a space for Q&A, conversations, announcements, reveals, and producer battles. It helps set the tone for the weekend and gives people a chance to speak on the culture directly.

The battle event is usually built around main-event battles and tournament-style competition. It gives dancers a serious stage to represent their name, team, borough, era, and style.

The awards ceremony is a full program with physical awards, performances, speeches, special moments, and public recognition. Winners are selected through the LiteFeet Awards panel system.

Dancer’s Choice Awards

Community-Voted Awards

The Dancer’s Choice Awards was created as a response to people feeling like they did not have enough say in the LiteFeet Awards process.

Instead of relying on a panel, Dancer’s Choice gives the community more direct control. People help choose the categories, submit nominees, and vote for the dancers, teams, producers, songs, battles, and moments they want to see recognized.

DCA is important because it captures how the community sees itself. It gives dancers and supporters a way to highlight favorites, overlooked names, viral moments, and categories that may not always fit inside a traditional awards panel.

Dancer’s Choice records live in their own section so the community-voted history stays separate from the LiteFeet Awards weekend records.
